Maildesk App Icon Variations

Five email-specific directions to replace the copied Today icon. Each keeps a desktop-app rounded-square silhouette and includes 64 px and 32 px previews.

HTML + inline SVG concepts

Folded Envelope

Most literal. Strong read at small sizes, with a warm notification mark that can become a product accent.

Inbox Tray

Feels more like a desktop mail utility. The tray shape stays recognizable when the detailed lines disappear.

Postmark Stamp

More distinctive and brandable. Good if Maildesk should feel crafted rather than generic.

Secure Mail

Leans into privacy and local-first expectations. Best direction if account/keychain work is core to the app.

Maildesk M

Least generic. Reads as an app brand first, with the envelope fold embedded in the monogram.

Six warm-paper icon directions

All options avoid the current generic purple-square look and use the app’s actual identity: paper surface, clay accent, daily list/calendar semantics, and warm Amigo neutrals.

A — Paper Checklist

most literal

Clear at small sizes: app = todo list. Warm paper, clay check, strong readability.

B — Today Calendar

date-forward

Says “daily app” immediately. Slight drawback: a fixed number is decorative, not live.

C — Clay Checkmark

boldest

Best dock visibility. More generic, but much less muddy than the current placeholder.

D — Stacked Days

carry-over

Hints at yesterday rolling into today. The most conceptually specific to this app.

E — Serif T

brand-like

More identity mark than utility icon. Elegant, but less self-explanatory.

F — Sunrise List

warmest

A softer “new day” metaphor. Pretty, slightly less crisp as a tiny dock icon.

My recommendation: A if you want the clearest functional icon; D if you want the app’s carry-over behavior encoded in the mark; C if dock visibility matters above all else.
